Astronava Account & Data Deletion Request

At Astronava, we respect your privacy and give you full control over your personal data. If you wish to delete your Astronava account and all associated data, you can do so easily using the methods outlined below.

Method 1: Delete Account Directly Inside the App (Recommended)

  1. Open the Astronava app on your device.
  2. Go to your Profile / Dashboard tab.
  3. Scroll down to the bottom and tap on "Delete Account".
  4. Confirm your deletion request. Your account and personal birth details will be permanently removed instantly.

Method 2: Request Deletion via Email

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request account and data deletion by contacting our support team:

Email: support@astronava.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Please include the registered email address associated with your Astronava account. Our team will process your request within 48 hours.

What Data is Deleted vs. Retained?

  • Data Permanently Deleted: Your user profile information, birth details (date, time, birthplace, latitude/longitude), saved Kundli charts, matchmaking history, and preferences.
  • Data Retained (if applicable): Transaction records related to physical store purchases may be retained for up to 30 days or as required by applicable tax and accounting laws, after which they are securely archived or anonymized.
